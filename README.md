## About this entry

The excuse does not blame traffic, the wifi or a sick pet — it
blames the meeting itself, in the language of resource management.
"Preserving the one resource it would have consumed" sounds like a
responsible decision from a careful manager, and then the resource
turns out to be the speaker's own will to attend the next meeting.
That twist is the whole laugh: the excuse admits, politely, that
the meeting was the problem, and that skipping it was an act of
self-care rather than laziness. Everybody who has sat through a
meeting that should have been an email recognises the feeling at
once, so the line needs no setup and no explanation. The program
holds the line, checks it against the two hundred character cap,
and writes it to /output/entry.txt exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one line to `/output/entry.txt`.
